Opportunity Map

Two distinct layers: Market Opportunities score Demand x Capability x Competition x Margin x Risk for a market/component pair, independent of any customer. Commercial Opportunities are specific customer deals moving through a sales pipeline. A commercial deal may trace back to the market opportunity that qualified it, but the two are never merged into one object.
