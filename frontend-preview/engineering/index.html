What to get right

  1. 01
    Current revision

    Name the released revision and keep superseded files out of the package.

  2. 02
    Critical features

    Mark interfaces, datums, and any acceptance-critical dimensions.

  3. 03
    Material and finish

    State the intended material and surface treatment or flag it as open.

  4. 04
    Quantity and timing

    Separate prototype, pilot, and repeat production intent.
