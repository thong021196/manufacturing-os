HOW A REQUIREMENT ENTERS THE SYSTEM

From an idea, a drawing, or a problem — to an accountable route.

  1. 01Understand

    CAD, drawing, BOM, or an unsolved problem is structured into an explicit manufacturing requirement — interfaces, datums, and acceptance criteria made explicit, not assumed.

  2. 02Route

    Geometry, material, quantity, and timeline are matched to a manufacturing route and a qualified partner in the network — not the first available quote.

  3. 03Produce & inspect

    Production is coordinated against the released revision, with inspection scoped to the features that actually control the outcome.

  4. 04Deliver with evidence

    The part ships with the record that proves it — measurement results, release evidence, and a revision history that survives the next order.
