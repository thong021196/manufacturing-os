# Search-Intent Pilot — Market Summary

Issue: #20 — Pass 6, Search-intent map + 35–50 page pilot architecture
Method: live WebSearch (US-localized) across 20 seed objects + 8 application areas + 6 problem/process areas, 50 initial candidate queries, **plus a focused evidence-strengthening pass (65 additional queries, Q051–Q115) that deepened the 13 P0/P1 physical-object pages only**, per the Human Gate audit review on PR #21. 115 real WebSearch queries were run in total. See `query-candidates.csv` for every query and its observed SERP composition, `intent-clusters.csv` for the deduped intents (including a `query_variation_check` column added in the fix pass), `pilot-pages.csv` for the proposed pages (including the `build_status` column added in the fix pass), and `cannibalization-map.csv` for merge/split decisions.

## Methodology note and honest limitation up front

The issue's research method suggests ~10–30 candidate queries *per sub* (which would be 340–1,020 queries across all seed subs). Given this session's tool budget, the initial pass ran **50 real WebSearch queries** — roughly 2–3 per object/application/problem area. The Human Gate audit correctly flagged this as materially below the issue's intended bar and asked for a *focused* deepening of the P0/P1 objects specifically, not a blanket expansion to 10–30/sub. The fix pass therefore ran **~5 additional distinct query framings for each of the 13 P0/P1 physical-object pages** (object, custom machining/fabrication, supplier, RFQ/quote, application, and lifecycle dimensions), for 65 new queries — bringing the total to **115 queries** across the whole pilot. P2/P3/experimental pages and cross-cutting application/problem-process pages were deliberately **not** re-queried in this fix pass (see `intent-clusters.csv`'s `query_variation_check` column for the explicit scope note on every row) — this keeps the deepening focused per the audit's own instruction rather than padding query count everywhere. Every claim in these deliverables traces to an actual `query_id` (Q001–Q115). Nothing about SERP composition, ranking domains, or intent is fabricated; "commercial_strength" and "SERP_fragmentation" are qualitative judgments from observed result types only — **no keyword-tool or Search-Console volume data was used or is claimed anywhere in this pass.**

The WebSearch tool used in this session is documented as US-localized ("Web search is only available in the US"). This pilot architecture is therefore based on **US evidence only**. AU-specific findings were explicitly out of scope for this fix pass (this session's tooling cannot obtain genuinely AU-localized evidence) and remain labeled **UNKNOWN** throughout, per the Human Gate audit's instruction — see the AU section below for the explicit decision.

## Deepened P0/P1 query pass — results (fix-pass addition)

The 13 P0/P1 object pages (`priority` = P0 or P1 in `pilot-pages.csv`, restricted to `page_type = object`) were each re-tested with ~5 new query framings. Full per-object detail is in `intent-clusters.csv`'s `query_variation_check` column and `query-candidates.csv` rows Q051–Q115. Summary:

- **10 of 13 P0/P1 objects confirmed cleanly across all 5 new framings**, with no wrong-buyer contamination: OBJ01 (joint housing), OBJ02 (actuator housing), OBJ05 (bearing housing/seat), OBJ07 (bracket/mounting adapter), OBJ08 (gripper fingers/jaws), OBJ09 (EOAT tooling plate), OBJ12 (enclosure/shell), OBJ13B (electronics enclosure), OBJ16 (sensor/camera/LiDAR mount), OBJ18 (ROV/pressure housing).
- **2 of 13 showed one phrasing-specific exception each**, without threatening the object's core validity: OBJ04 (robot shaft — the bare phrase "custom robot drive shaft fabrication service" pulls automotive driveshaft/prop-shaft contamination, the same cross-industry-noise pattern already known from "welded frame"; the CNC-turning-qualified and RFQ/application/lifecycle framings all stayed clean) and OBJ10 (robot base/pedestal — "robot base supplier CNC welding" collapses into welding-*robot* sellers, a keyword-ambiguity issue, not a wrong-buyer-vertical issue; the RFQ/application/lifecycle framings all stayed clean).
- **1 of 13 (OBJ14, robot chassis) showed material exceptions on 3 of its 5 new framings** — automotive/hot-rod chassis-builder contamination (repeating the original Q031 pattern under new wording), welding-robot-seller contamination (same ambiguity as OBJ10), and near-total absence of genuine fabrication-to-drawing content under a "mobile robot chassis machining service" framing (reinforcing the pre-existing finished-AMR-platform buyer-segmentation risk). OBJ14 remains a real, evidenced object (its RFQ-quote and lifecycle framings were clean), but it is the **most fragile SERP identity of the 13 P0/P1 objects tested** and its `build_status` is set to `validate_first` rather than `validated` as a result.
- **A new, previously-undocumented contamination pattern was found**: placing the bare word "welding" next to "robot" in a supplier-framed query collapses into welding-*robot* sellers (companies that sell robots which weld) rather than fabricators of parts *for* robots. This was confirmed independently on two P0/P1 objects (OBJ10 and OBJ14) and is now documented as a query-hygiene rule for this pilot (see `cannibalization-map.csv`).
- **One incidental positive finding**: a narrower cobot-specific query ("cobot mounting bracket machining service", "cobot EOAT tooling plate machining") surfaced genuine cobot-scoped commercial content that the original contaminated cobot query (Q038) missed. This refines but does not overturn APP02's experimental/deferred status — the real signal is for mounting/tooling hardware already covered by OBJ07/OBJ09, not a distinct standalone "custom parts for cobots" vertical.

## Which physical objects show the clearest procurement/RFQ search intent

Strongest, cleanest evidence (fragmented SERP, specialist manufacturers visible, no giant-platform or off-topic contamination, real technical content available):

- **Gripper fingers/jaws** (OBJ08) — best evidence in the whole pass, and CONFIRMED again across all 5 deepened query framings (Q071–Q075). A dedicated online CAD-to-part configurator (GripShape) exists purely for this object, plus Kurt Workholding, Stryver, Rohtek, zintilon. **Important distinction**: this is strong evidence of commercial supply/search-surface existence for this object — a specialist manufacturer judged it worth building dedicated sales infrastructure around — not proof of buyer demand *volume*. No click-through, conversion, or search-volume data was available in this pass to quantify demand; the claim here is limited to "this object supports a real, live commercial offering," which is the correct and sufficient bar for a search-intent pilot page.
- **EOAT tooling plates & parts** (OBJ09) — mature, purely specialist B2B niche (EOAT Machining, EMI Corp, CRG, Millibar, ASS EOAT). No giant-platform or hobby-retail noise anywhere.
- **Robot shaft** (OBJ04) — fragmented, specialist-dominated, real technical depth (Swiss turning, runout/concentricity).
- **Robot base/pedestal** (OBJ10) — fragmented specialist fabricators plus one legitimate scaled competitor (Vention), real welding+machining technical depth.
- **ROV/pressure housing** (OBJ18) — cleanly specialist, titanium/pressure-vessel technical depth, zero giant-platform or off-topic noise.
- **Custom CNC-machining-qualified framings of joint housing, actuator housing, bracket, sensor/camera mount** (OBJ01, OBJ02, OBJ07, OBJ16) — all show fragmented, specialist-dominated SERPs *once the query includes "custom CNC machining"* (see contamination note below).

## Where broad queries are dominated by large platforms

- **Broad "custom manufacturing RFQ CAD quote China supplier robotics parts"** (Q050): unambiguous sweep by Alibaba (cited with 200,000+ suppliers), Xometry, Haizol (cited with 300,000+ users), MFG.com. This is the clearest giant-platform-dominance evidence in the pass — directly confirms the issue's own caution against broad category pages.
- **"Industrial robot custom parts machining manufacturer"** (Q040): dominated by scaled on-demand manufacturing aggregator platforms (Xometry, Fictiv, Hubs/Protolabs Network) that each run a dedicated `/robotics/` vertical page. These aren't Alibaba/Amazon-style marketplaces, but they occupy the same competitive role — big, well-funded, broad-catalog, hard to outrank on the head term.
- **"Custom robot enclosure shell manufacturer"** (Q026): one Alibaba product-detail page ranks directly in organic results — the only query in this pass where a marketplace *listing itself* (not just a directory/aggregator) appeared.
- **"AMR custom parts manufacturing CNC"** (Q039): collapsed entirely into generic multi-industry CNC-quoting platforms (eBay, Xometry, PCBWay, eMachineShop) with zero AMR-specific content at all — see "what should not become a page" below.

## Where specialist manufacturers rank (fragmentation confirmed) — and validation of the "object-first + machining qualifier" pattern

For nearly every object once framed with "custom CNC machining"/"custom fabrication" rather than a bare noun or "manufacturer," the SERP is dominated by a long tail of small-to-mid specialist CNC job shops, many China-based, several of which have already built dedicated content-marketing pages *per object* — e.g. zintilon.com runs separate pages for shafts, bearing housings, sensor housings, and custom grippers; RivCut runs separate pages (some templated per-city) for actuator housings, gripper components, robot-arm links, and defense-optics/sensor parts. **This is itself evidence that specialist manufacturers already treat object-first content as a viable commercial-supply strategy** — competitors have independently converged on splitting content by physical object rather than by process × material × robot-type, which is the architecture the issue asks us to validate. (As above: this is evidence of supply/search-surface behavior by competing manufacturers, not a direct measurement of buyer demand volume.)

**Validated after the deepened P0/P1 pass**: of the 13 P0/P1 objects re-tested with query variation (see the "Deepened P0/P1 query pass" section above), **10 objects confirm the object-first + machining/fabrication-qualifier pattern cleanly across every new framing** (OBJ01, OBJ02, OBJ05, OBJ07, OBJ08, OBJ09, OBJ12, OBJ13B, OBJ16, OBJ18). **2 objects confirm the pattern overall but with one phrasing-specific exception each** (OBJ04 shaft, OBJ10 base/pedestal — see above). **1 object (OBJ14, chassis) is a genuine exception**: even with the machining/fabrication qualifier present, "chassis" as a term is overloaded enough (automotive aftermarket, welding-robot sellers, finished-AMR-platform OEMs) that 3 of its 5 new query framings returned contaminated results. The pattern therefore holds for **12 of 13 (92%) P0/P1 objects**, with chassis as the one object where the qualifier alone is insufficient and the page must rely on much stricter on-page disambiguation rather than title/keyword framing alone.

## A recurring and important contamination pattern: bare object/manufacturer queries pull the wrong buyer

This was the single most consistent finding across the pass, observed independently for at least 4 different objects:

1. **"Manufacturer" queries for assembled components collapse toward the assembled-component seller, not the machining-service shop.** "Robot joint housing manufacturer," "robot actuator housing manufacturer," and "harmonic reducer housing manufacturer" all returned sellers of the *complete integrated component* (motor+gearbox+encoder joint modules, harmonic-drive gearboxes) rather than shops that would machine a housing to the buyer's own drawing. (Q002, Q005, Q007)
2. **Bare "bracket" and "motor mount" queries are captured by hobby/education/FRC-competition retail**, not industrial RFQ buyers at all. "Robot bracket" (Q017) and "robot motor mount manufacturer" (Q014) returned VEX Robotics, REV Robotics/goBILDA, RobotShop, SuperDroid, FingerTech — zero industrial CNC content in either search.
3. **Bare "chassis" + "welded frame" pulls in automotive aftermarket chassis builders** (hot-rod/race-car shops) as cross-industry noise (Q031).
4. **"Battery enclosure" collapses into full battery-PACK manufacturers** (cell + BMS + certification), a fundamentally different supplier vertical from mechanical-housing-only CNC/sheet-metal shops (Q028).

**Implication for the pilot:** every object page's target keyword phrasing and H1 must include the machining/custom-service qualifier ("custom CNC machining," "custom fabrication") and must never rely on the bare object noun or the bare "+ manufacturer" phrasing alone — those forms measurably pull a different, non-RFQ buyer.

## US vs AU — explicit decision: AU is UNKNOWN / out-of-scope for page-architecture prioritization (fix-pass addition)

The Human Gate audit on PR #21 correctly noted that only Q049 is an AU-demand query, and that this session's WebSearch tool is documented as US-localized, so AU evidence was too thin to responsibly influence page architecture. This fix pass did **not** attempt to obtain new AU-localized evidence (the tool available in this session has no AU-geolocation option, so a differently-worded query would not have changed that constraint) and instead makes the explicit choice the audit asked for:

**Decision: this pilot architecture is based on US evidence only. AU page-prioritization is marked UNKNOWN / out of scope for this pilot.** No page's priority, `build_status`, or inclusion in the 34-page architecture is based on AU-specific evidence anywhere in this folder. Where AU is mentioned below, it is for transparency about what was (and was not) found — not as input to any architecture decision.

- **China-side supply signal for AU** (informational only, not used for prioritization): Chinese contract manufacturers do explicitly claim to ship to/serve Australia (Q048: Senze Precision, VMT, Xinqida, HLH Rapid all mention Australia among served regions). This is consistent with issue #18/#19's conclusion that supply-side existence is already established, but it says nothing about AU buyer search behavior.
- **AU-side signal is genuinely thin and different in character from the US signal** (informational only). Searching "custom robot parts manufacturer Australia" (Q049) — the only AU-qualified query in this pass, now or after the fix — did **not** surface AU-based specialist CNC-for-robot-parts competitors comparable to the US/China cluster. It surfaced Australian **robot-arm integrators** (Automated Solutions Australia, The Robot Factory — a different business than machining a customer's own part design) and **hobby/education retail** (Robot Gear Australia, Core Electronics, eBay Australia). One plausible analog was found — compass-anvil.com — but not investigated further.
- **Do not infer US/AU parity or divergence from this one query.** One AU-qualified query cannot support a conclusion either way about AU demand, and none is drawn here. A genuinely AU-localized research pass (AU-geolocated search tool, or AU keyword-volume/GSC data) is required before any AU-specific page, AU-only content, or AU-weighted prioritization is considered — not a follow-up phrasing of the same US-localized tool.
- No AU-specific SERP differences were found for any *object* query (all 115 object-level queries in this pass, both the original 50 and the deepened 65, used US-localized search per the `market` column in `query-candidates.csv` — only Q048/Q049 ever targeted AU framing, and neither was repeated or expanded in the fix pass, consistent with keeping AU explicitly out of scope).

## What should NOT become a page (this pass's recommendation)

- **Bare "robot bracket" / "robot motor mount manufacturer"** as a page's primary target phrase — hobby/FRC-retail contaminated (see contamination pattern above). Build the object pages, but title/target them around "custom CNC machining," never the bare noun.
- **"AMR custom parts manufacturing"** as a literal phrase — zero AMR-specific SERP identity found (Q039); reframe as chassis-led (see OBJ14/APP03).
- **A standalone "robot parts inspection / CMM report" page** — Q046 showed no distinct buyer intent separate from generic CMM-equipment vendors and CMM-machine-tending automation content. Fold sample inspection-report content into each object page as a trust section instead.
- **The broad "custom manufacturing RFQ CAD quote China supplier robotics parts" phrase** as a primary SEO target — unambiguously Alibaba/Xometry/Haizol/MFG.com-dominated (Q050). A related informational page (PRB_E) is still proposed, but scoped to the how-to/qualification angle, not this broad commercial phrase.
- **Permutation pages** (object × city, object × material, object × process) — RivCut's own competitor pattern (templated near-identical pages for "gripper components," "robot arm links," and "actuator housings" repeated across many US cities: Milwaukee, Birmingham, Atlanta, Chicago, Sacramento, Des Moines, Dallas, Seattle) is flagged explicitly in this research as an example of the exact failure mode the issue warns against. It is documented as a cautionary competitor observation, **not** a pattern to copy, and no city-permutation pages are proposed here.
- **A separate LiDAR-mount page** — no distinct commercial content found apart from generic sensor/camera-mount content (Q034); merged into OBJ16.

APP02's status is now slightly better-informed (see the cobot nuance in the deepened-pass section above and in `cannibalization-map.csv`) but the finding is narrow (mounting/tooling hardware, already covered elsewhere) and does not justify promoting APP02 to a standalone page. APP05 was not re-queried this pass (P3-experimental, out of scope).
- **AU demand-side signal** — explicitly marked UNKNOWN / out of scope for page-architecture prioritization; see the AU section above for the full decision. Not re-tested in this fix pass by design (no AU-localized tooling available).
- **Harmonic reducer/drive housing (OBJ03), motor mount (OBJ06), structural/arm-link parts (OBJ11), robot-cell jig/fixture (OBJ19), and prototype multi-part assembly (OBJ20)** — all P2, not re-queried this pass, all carry `build_status = validate_first` in `pilot-pages.csv` reflecting their originally thin/bundled standalone evidence; monitor before promoting to independent top-level pages or investing full content.
- **No search-volume, click-through, or ranking-difficulty data of any kind was available in this session** (no Keyword Planner/Ahrefs/SEMrush/GSC access, consistent with the task's constraints). - **WebFetch reliability**: this session relied entirely on WebSearch's own result snippets (titles/URLs/summary text) rather than full-page fetches, consistent with prior sessions in this repo noting WebFetch is unreliable/blocked for several domains (including alibaba.com). ## Final summary (Human Gate fix pass, PR #21)

- **Query count**: 115 total real WebSearch queries (50 from the original pass + 65 from the deepened P0/P1 pass, Q051–Q115). No queries were fabricated or padded to hit a target number.
- **P0/P1 objects with multi-query confirmation**: 13 of 13 P0/P1 object pages received the deepened ~5-query pass. **12 of 13 (92%) confirm their page-level intent survives query variation** (10 cleanly across all 5 new framings; 2 with one phrasing-specific exception each that does not threaten the object's core validity). **1 of 13 (OBJ14, chassis) shows material contamination on 3 of 5 new framings** and is the one clear exception — real object, fragile SERP identity, downgraded to `build_status = validate_first`.
- **Object-first + machining/fabrication-qualifier pattern**: validated for 12 of 13 (92%) P0/P1 objects; OBJ14 (chassis) is the documented exception.
- **Page count**: unchanged at **34 total candidate architecture pages** (not padded toward 35–50; see `pilot-architecture.md`).
- **Build-readiness split** (new `build_status` field in `pilot-pages.csv`): **19 validated**, **13 validate_first**, **2 defer** (APP02, APP05) — 19 of 34 (56%) are validated/build-ready today; the remaining 15 either need a further validation pass (13) or stay explicitly deferred/experimental (2). Total candidate-architecture pages (34) and validated build-ready pages (19) are two different numbers and should not be conflated.
- **Unresolved cannibalization pairs**: 3 pairs remain explicitly unresolved / flagged for ongoing query hygiene rather than fully closed: (1) OBJ14 vs. automotive/hot-rod chassis builders (durable contamination, confirmed twice under different phrasings), (2) OBJ10 + OBJ14 vs. welding-robot sellers (new keyword-ambiguity finding from this fix pass), and (3) APP02 vs. OBJ07/OBJ09 cobot-mounting content (narrowed, not resolved — APP02 stays deferred). All other cannibalization-map rows carry a firm decision (merge / parent-child / separate).
- **Unsourced technical specs neutralized**: 6 numeric tolerance/fit claims that traced only to a single vendor's marketing page (or to no traceable prior source at all) were replaced with "drawing-specific / source required before publication" in `pilot-pages.csv` and `intent-clusters.csv`: OBJ01/INT-01 bearing-bore concentricity (was ~0.0002in), OBJ03/INT-03 pilot-fit clearance (was 0.025mm — the H6/H7 fit-class terminology itself was kept, since it traces to Harmonic Drive's own catalog documentation), OBJ05/INT-05 bearing-seat coaxiality (was ≤0.003mm), OBJ08/INT-08 critical grip-surface/pin-pivot tolerance (was ±0.0005in), OBJ16/INT-16 true-position tolerance (was ±0.05mm — traceable sources gave inconsistent single-vendor figures from ±0.01mm to ±0.05mm), and PRB_C/INT-P03's generic tolerance threshold (was "sub-0.01mm"). These were checked against `docs/research/robotics-rfq-anatomy/` and `docs/research/robotics-rfq-family-expansion/` (available on their own unmerged branches, `claude/issue-13-robot-rfq-anatomy-research` and `claude/issue-15-rfq-family-expansion-research`) plus this pass's own 115 searches; none had a `documentation_verified`-grade source for the specific number, only `supplier_marketing`-grade single-vendor claims or no traceable source at all.
- **AU evidence handling**: explicitly labeled UNKNOWN / out of scope for this pilot's page-architecture decisions (see the AU section above). No new AU-localized evidence was obtained (this session's tooling has no AU-geolocation option); the pilot architecture is based on US evidence only, per the Human Gate audit's option (b).
