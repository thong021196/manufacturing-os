## Process capabilities (declared)

CNC machining and sheet metal fabrication (laser cutting/profiling, CNC
press-brake bending, welded assemblies), plus SLA/3D printing and
low-volume vacuum-casting moulding as separate service lines. One of the
few suppliers in this dataset with **both** CNC and sheet-metal offered
directly. [3D Prototyping — CNC Machining](https://3dprototyping.com.au/cnc-machining) [3D Prototyping — Sheet Metal](https://3dprototyping.com.au/sheet-metal)

## Suitability for prototype/low-volume robotics work

**Structurally one of the best-positioned Australian suppliers in this
set** — genuinely dual-process (CNC + sheet metal under one roof, useful
for a robotics order needing both a machined bracket and a formed
enclosure) and explicit no-MOQ prototype positioning directly matches the
wedge's low-volume, high-mix profile. The major weakness is that almost
no quantitative capability data (tolerance, lead time, certification) is
published, so this fit judgment rests more on business-model match than
on verified technical specification — a stronger candidate for direct
follow-up/verification than for immediate reliance.
