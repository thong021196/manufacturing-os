## Important framing note

Like Additive Engineering Australia, Formero's primary declared
positioning is **3D printing / additive manufacturing** — describing
itself as "Australia's largest 3D printing facility" — with CNC
machining and injection molding/tooling offered as additional service
lines rather than the core business. [Formero — 3D Printing Services Melbourne](https://formero.com.au/3d-printing-services-melbourne/)

## Process capabilities (declared)

CNC machining (plastic and metal parts). Injection moulding & tooling
(CAD design, CAM, single & multi-cavity tools, Moldflow analysis, insert
& overmoulding, finishing). Six additive manufacturing technologies
in-house (primary business line). [Formero — CNC Machining](https://formero.com.au/cnc-machining/) [Formero — Injection Moulding & Tooling](https://formero.com.au/injection-moulding-tooling/)

## Certifications (declared, self-reported, not independently verified)

**ISO 9001:2015 certified.** Notably, the certification is described as
having been "accelerated as a result of a grant awarded by the Centre for
Defence Industry Capability, in recognition of Formero's potential to
manufacture Aerospace and Defence components in-country" — this ties the

statement, though it was not independently confirmed against the granting
