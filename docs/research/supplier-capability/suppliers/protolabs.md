## QC / inspection capability (declared)

All machined parts are inspected for cosmetic conformance to workmanship
standards and dimensional conformance on at least three dimensions
(typically X, Y, Z); production parts can get First Article Inspection
(FAI) and Certificate of Compliance (CoC) documentation. [Protolabs — CNC Machining Quality](https://www.protolabs.com/services/cnc-machining/quality/)

## Quantity / MOQ / prototype-low-volume fit (declared)

CNC machining spans "smaller quantities" up to volumes of one million
parts (i.e., prototype-through-mass-production positioning, not a
prototype-floor-specific number). Injection molding / LSR is offered at
25 to 10,000+ pieces low-volume quantities. [Protolabs — CNC Machining](https://www.protolabs.com/services/cnc-machining/) [Protolabs — Digital Manufacturing FAQs](https://www.protolabs.com/resources/faqs/)

## Lead-time claims

"Lead times in as fast as 1 day... on eligible 3D-printed, machined, and
injection-molded parts." Injection molding production standard lead time
is 10 days, expeditable to as fast as 1 day depending on part. [Protolabs — Digital Manufacturing FAQs](https://www.protolabs.com/resources/faqs/)

## Certifications (declared, self-reported, not independently verified)

ISO 9001:2015, ISO 13485, AS9100D, ITAR registered. [Protolabs — ISO/AS9100D Certification](https://www.protolabs.com/iso/) [Protolabs — Aerospace CNC Machining](https://www.protolabs.com/services/cnc-machining/aerospace/)
