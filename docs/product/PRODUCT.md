# Product Definition

## Purpose
Manufacturing OS is an operating system for discovering manufacturing demand, mapping supplier capability, converting qualified demand into RFQs, normalizing quotes, tracking production/QC, and feeding real outcomes back into the system.

## Core loop
`MARKET DEMAND + SUPPLIER CAPABILITY → OPPORTUNITY MAP → ONTOLOGY → SEARCH SURFACES → CAD/RFQ → SUPPLIER QUOTE → CUSTOMER QUOTE → ORDER → PRODUCTION → QC → OUTCOME → BETTER DEMAND + BETTER SUPPLY → LOOP`

## Initial wedge
Custom mechanical parts for robotics and automation, beginning with parts such as actuator housings, gripper jaws, sensor mounts, motor mounts, EOAT plates, brackets, housings, jigs and inspection fixtures.

## Initial commercial flow
1. Buyer discovers a relevant search surface.
2. Buyer uploads CAD/drawing/BOM and requirements.
3. Team reviews manually.
4. RFQ is normalized.
5. Matching suppliers are selected manually using capability data.
6. Supplier quotes are normalized.
7. Customer receives one commercial quote.
8. Accepted work moves through production, QC and delivery.
9. Results are stored and feed future routing, search, and supplier intelligence.

## Initial market scope
- United States
- Australia

## MVP goal
Prove the full acquisition and transaction chain:
`Search → Qualified visitor → CAD upload → Qualified RFQ → Quote → Order`

## Not MVP
- Marketplace
- Full supplier portal
- Large customer portal
- Autonomous purchasing
- Fully automated release gate
- Automated supplier routing without human review
- Shopify integration before repeat transaction volume justifies it
- Broad multi-country expansion
